# Nebulynk Trademark Policy

This policy explains how the Nebulynk name, word mark, logos, visual identity,
and confusingly similar marks may be used.

The Nebulynk marks identify software and services from Alexander Zerling. The
software license grants copyright permissions for the code, but it does not
grant trademark rights except where expressly stated.

## Uses That Are Allowed

You may use the Nebulynk name without prior written permission when the use is
truthful, non-confusing, and does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or an
official partnership. Examples include:

- Referring to the original Nebulynk project.
- Linking to Nebulynk or describing compatibility with Nebulynk.
- Writing reviews, tutorials, documentation, integration guides, or comparison
  materials.
- Including Nebulynk in software catalogs, app catalogs, package indexes,
  deployment templates, one-click installers, or similar discovery and
  deployment directories when the listing itself is not a paid
  Nebulynk-specific commercial offer.
- Providing deployment facilitation, automated provisioning, deployment
  templates, one-click installers, update automation, and separately itemized
  or generally marked-up infrastructure billing, as long as the offer is not
  marketed as official Nebulynk, Nebulynk Cloud, Managed Nebulynk, or a
  Nebulynk-specific hosted or managed service.
- Stating that an unmodified distribution is "Nebulynk" if it is clearly
  identified as the original project.

Catalog and deployment-template listings are welcome, including listings in
third-party tools and app catalogs, as long as the listing is accurate, does not
look official unless approved, and does not sell Nebulynk-specific hosting,
management, paid placement, or paid services without a written partnership
agreement.

General cloud, infrastructure, and deployment-platform fees are fine. A fee
becomes Nebulynk-specific when it is priced or marketed around Nebulynk users,
seats, workspaces, tenants, messages, features, official support, operations,
service-level commitments, backups, monitoring, or incident response.

## Uses That Need Permission

Please get prior written permission before using the Nebulynk marks for:

- A paid hosted, managed, or SaaS offering based on Nebulynk.
- Paid Nebulynk-specific support, operations, training, marketplace placement,
  resale, or consulting offers.
- A deployment offer marketed as Nebulynk Cloud, Managed Nebulynk, official
  Nebulynk hosting, or a substantially similar Nebulynk-specific service.
- A product, service, company, domain, social account, package, or app listing
  whose name could make users think it is official Nebulynk.
- Merchandise, events, advertising, or sponsorship materials.
- Modified versions that are distributed or offered as a product or service
  under the Nebulynk name.

## Forks And Modified Versions

You may say that your project is "based on Nebulynk" or "compatible with
Nebulynk" if that is accurate. If you distribute a modified version as a product
or service, use a clearly different name and branding so users can tell it is
not the official Nebulynk project.

## Prohibited Uses

Do not use the Nebulynk marks in a way that is misleading, confusing, unlawful,
or damaging to the reputation of the project. Do not register domains, package
names, app names, company names, or social accounts that impersonate Nebulynk or
make an unofficial product look official.
